# STEP-18: QR code to share the app

- **Management → Compartir la app:** a large QR code for the app link, with the club logo in the center (on green), plus buttons **Descargar QR (PNG)**, **Imprimir cartel** (an A5/A4 sheet: logo, "Descarga la app del club", QR, and 3 short install steps, in Spanish and English), and **Copiar enlace / Compartir por WhatsApp**.
- **Landing page on desktop:** next to the download buttons, a small QR with "Escanéalo con tu celular". On phones it's hidden (they're already on the phone).
- Generate the QR in the app (no external service). It must scan reliably, so keep high error correction with the logo in the center.

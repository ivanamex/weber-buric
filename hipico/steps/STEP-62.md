# STEP-62: the footer course, so the horse really jumps

Ivana (15 Oct, the footer of `/`): the letter-horse "flies" high above the fences, far from the ground line, much bigger than the fences, and doesn't read as jumping anything. The slogan is huge and sits right under the nav bar. The STEP-53 course was never checked as a whole.

## The course (`RunningHorse course` + the footer band)
- **Scale:** the horse is about 1.4× the fence height (today it's about 3×). Fences stay as drawn; the horse shrinks so its legs and head stay readable.
- **On the ground:** between jumps, the hooves run **on the dotted ground line**.
- **Each jump:** take-off about one horse-length before the fence. The arc peaks only a little above the top pole (about 20% of the fence height), then the horse lands one horse-length after it. Head up at take-off, legs tucked over the fence, front legs reaching at landing (pose per phase, not one stretched pose for the whole flight).
- **Rhythm:** canter in, jump 1, canter, jump 2, canter, jump 3, canter out of the frame on the right. About 9 s per loop, then a 2 s pause, then it starts again from the left. It only starts when the band is on screen.
- **Size of the band:** about 260 px tall on a computer and 180 px on phones. Phones show 2 fences, not 3.
- Reduced motion: the horse stands at the start line, with no loop.

## The slogan
- "Tu lugar feliz tiene caballos." at 56 px on a computer (40 px on phones), not full-width display size.
- 96 px of space between the last section and the slogan, and 32 px between the slogan and the course.
- Centered, olivewood.

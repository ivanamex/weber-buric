# STEP-16: active plan front and center, change plan, payment history, password nudge

## 1. "Plan activo" always visible
- At the top of the family **Inicio** and **Mi plan** screens: a compact card showing **Plan activo · 8 clases/mes**, the horseshoe counter (5 of 8), "Se renueva el 31 oct", and the status (Pagado / Por revisar / Pendiente).
- Two buttons on the card: **Reservar clase** (coral glass) and **Cambiar plan** (secondary).
- Families with no plan see "Aún no tienes plan" + **Elegir plan**.

## 2. Change plan (gentle, honest upgrade)
- **Cambiar plan** opens the plans side by side, with the current one marked "Tu plan".
- Under each plan, show the price per class, e.g. "$550 por clase", plus on bigger plans a small line "Ahorras 15% por clase". No pressure copy, just the numbers.
- **Upgrade:** it applies right away, and the family pays only the difference for the current month (transfer with receipt, or card when available); the extra classes are added as soon as it's approved.
- **Downgrade:** it applies from the next renewal date, and the app says so ("Tu nuevo plan empieza el 1 nov").
- Management sees plan changes in Cobros (the difference to collect) and in Familias (plan history).

## 3. Payment history
- In **Mi plan**, a **Historial de pagos** section: date, concept (plan, pensión, camp, difference), amount, method, status, and a link to the receipt when there is one. Newest first, the last 12 months.
- Management sees the same history inside each family in Familias.

## 4. Password nudge after signing in by email link
- When someone signs in through the email link in a browser on an iPhone (not the installed app), show one friendly card right after login: "Crea una contraseña para abrir la app instalada sin esperar el código", with a password field and **Guardar**, plus "Ahora no".
- Show it once per device. Don't show it if they already have a password.

# STEP-10: Horario (class calendar) in management

- New **Horario** tab in management: a weekly view. Create and edit a class (day, time, duration, level, instructor, arena, capacity), and turn it on or off.
- **Cancel a single date** (rain, an event): booked families see "Clase cancelada", and the class goes back to their plan.
- Manage **instructors** and **horses** from the same area: school horses, and boarded horses with their owner family.
- **Hoy** keeps Vino / No vino (a no-show counts as a used class). Add a **"Todos vinieron"** button per class, so one tap marks the whole group.
